About

I’m Andrii Shnayder, a UI/UX designer in Tallinn. My concepts don’t stay in Figma — they get built.

How I work

  • I break a standard when my eye says it doesn’t fit — and I can tell you why.
  • Every line must be readable: colour, size and the space around it.
  • Minimal and specific to the task. No boxes for the sake of boxes.
  • A simple task gets the same care as a 3D showpiece.

Questions you might have

No commercial experience?
Not yet. Every project here is self-initiated: unofficial redesigns for real companies, a study assignment and my own app. I build them as working sites, so you can judge how they behave, not only how they look.
Can you code?
Enough to take my concepts to working Shopify themes. I’m studying programming alongside design.

Why did this page change colour?
Its colours follow the sky over Tallinn: light by day, warm when the sun is low, blue hour, then night. In December that means low sun all day and dark by half past three. You can fix it to day or night at the bottom of any page.
