How I work

  • Start from a question a real person asks, in their words.
  • Look at what exists and write down why it is that way before changing it.
  • Build the thing, not a picture of it: Figma for the system, code for the behaviour.
  • Say plainly what is a fact, what is a guess and what I have not tested.
