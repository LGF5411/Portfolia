PULSE — my own Windows app, 2026

A game booster that tells you the risk before it touches your PC

My part
Product, design, build
Platform
Windows desktop app
Status
In development, v0.5

What it is

My own Windows app for gamers: game boost, a cleaner, startup apps and performance checks — plus an assistant that knows one subject only: making your PC faster without harming it. Each tab does one job.

Rules I broke

  1. Usual rule: A booster has a big round BOOST button.
    PULSE has no magic button.

    Every fix shows what it gives, the risk, whether it’s worth it on this PC, how to do it and how to undo it. You decide with the facts in front of you.

  2. Usual rule: Let the AI decide.
    The assistant can’t do anything you haven’t confirmed.

    It runs only whitelisted PULSE functions, one confirmation each, with a backup first. The risk level of every action is set by PULSE’s code, not by the AI.

  3. Usual rule: Promise more FPS.
    Some fixes are marked “try with measurement”.

    PULSE measures before and after, and offers to undo the change if it didn’t help.

What it will never do

Disable Windows Defender, remove the Store, Edge WebView2 or Xbox, apply registry “FPS tweaks” or switch off services in bulk.

Where it stands

In development. Version 0.5 has a restore point, an activity log and undo for every fix. The assistant works offline for now.
