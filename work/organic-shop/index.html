What it is

A redesign of organic-shop.com, made for the company itself. I built it as a working store rather than a set of pictures, hoping they would hire me. Organic Shop 3D is the final version.

Rules I broke

  1. Usual rule: A concept is a beautiful mockup.
    This concept is a store that works.

    It’s a real Shopify theme, so the company can judge it by clicking through it, not by imagining it.

  2. Usual rule: Holiday banners use renders.
    The gift sets are the real boxes.

    The New Year banners use Organic Shop’s actual gift-set boxes in their original proportions, so they read as photos of what you will get.

Where it stands

Finished. I don’t keep a public copy online — ask me and I’ll walk you through it.
