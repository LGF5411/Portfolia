01The question

One banner is never one banner.

A café makes a nice square post. Then Google wants a 728 × 90 strip, Instagram wants a story, and the printer wants a poster. The same picture gets squeezed, the headline stops fitting, the button disappears.

02What I decided

  1. 1

    The first screen is the product working

    Scroll, and one banner turns into a leaderboard, a square, a story, a billboard, and then lays out all 26 sizes. No claim about quality, just the thing happening.

  2. 2

    Your words, immediately

    A field under the banner says “Your headline”. Type your own offer and every size on the page shows it.

  3. 3

    Sell only what can be promised

    Six services with a price and a date each, and a section called “What we don’t take on”. A new studio earns trust faster by refusing than by listing everything.

  4. 4

    Prices and dates on the page

    “48 h to the first sketch”, “12 sizes for €390”. Nobody has to write an e-mail to learn whether they can afford it.

  5. 5

    A free test instead of a client list

    A new studio has no cases to show. So the offer is: send one banner, get three sizes back, free. The work is the reference.

  6. 6

    A brief that answers back

    Five short questions, and the price and the date appear while you fill them in, before you send anything.

03The screens

04What is real and what is not

It is a business on day one, and the page says so.

The site is live in English, Estonian and Russian. The offer, the prices and the free test are real. There are no client cases yet, the example businesses are made up and labelled, and the Estonian text is still waiting for a careful read.
